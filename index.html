What is Avalanche Staking?

Core Stake is the Core web destination for delegating AVAX or validating on Avalanche, with staking activity handled through the P-Chain. The Avalanche Builder Hub documents the minimums, lock terms, and reward rules.

How Avalanche Staking works

  1. Connect CoreOpen Core Stake and connect a Core-compatible wallet before reviewing balances and chain location.
  2. Move to P-ChainIf your AVAX is on the C-Chain, use the cross-chain transfer flow so it is available for staking.
  3. Choose your roleDelegate at least 25 AVAX to an active validator, or validate with a prepared node and at least 2,000 AVAX.
  4. Review and signCheck validator, amount, reward address, and term before signing; confirmed staking parameters cannot be changed early.

Before you stake

Built for Core

Core Stake supports validation, delegation, and X/P/C cross-chain transfers directly in Core web, as described in Core support.

P-Chain native

Staking uses AVAX on the Platform Chain, not the C-Chain DeFi environment, so chain placement matters before signing.

Term locked

Mainnet staking terms run from 2 weeks to 1 year, and rewards are paid at the end if protocol conditions are met.

Avalanche Staking FAQ

What is avalanche staking in Core?

It is the Core web flow for placing AVAX on the P-Chain as a delegator or validator, helping secure Avalanche through a self-custody wallet workflow.

What are the avalanche staking minimums?

Avalanche mainnet requires at least 25 AVAX to delegate and 2,000 AVAX to validate, according to the official staking parameters.

Can avalanche staking be withdrawn early?

No. Once a staking transaction is confirmed, the amount, validator, reward address, and term cannot be changed until the selected period ends.

Is avalanche staking done on the C-Chain?

No. Staking occurs on the P-Chain; Core can help move AVAX between the C-Chain, P-Chain, and X-Chain before you stake.

Does avalanche staking have slashing?

Avalanche documentation states there is no protocol slashing, but a validator that fails uptime requirements can result in zero rewards for that staking period.
